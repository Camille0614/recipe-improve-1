French Toast

Crispy outside, soft inside, with sweet strawberry jam and creamy peanut butter tucked in the middle.

Let's Cook →

About This Recipe

French toast is a classic breakfast loved all over the world. In this version, we sandwich strawberry jam and peanut butter between two slices of bread, dip it in egg, and fry it until golden. Finish with a drizzle of honey. Simple and cozy!

Time:

15 minutes

Serves:

1 person

Level:

Easy
